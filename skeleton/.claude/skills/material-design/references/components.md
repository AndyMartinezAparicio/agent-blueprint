"Flutter map" lines are project judgment (the repo does not document Flutter). {{NOMBRE_PROYECTO}}'s `project-ui` and DESIGN.md "Notas del proyecto" override anything here. Do not add components that `docs/` does not call for.

## Buttons
Source: .../components/CommonButton.md (Button.md is only an index)
- Five styles in order of emphasis: elevated, filled, tonal, outlined, text.
- Filled = most prominent after the FAB; for final or unblocking actions (Save, Confirm). Default style.
- Tonal = less prominent than filled, still for final actions needing less emphasis (e.g. Next in onboarding); middle ground between filled and outlined.
- Outlined = actions that need attention but are not primary (See all); also the "change your mind / escape" action. Pairs with filled as the secondary action.
- Text = low emphasis, e.g. when presenting multiple options.
- Elevated = outlined with a shadow; only when absolutely necessary (button over a patterned background) to avoid "shadow creep".
- Icons go on the leading side, before the label.
- Toggle buttons: binary selections (Save, Favorite); outlined icon when unselected, filled icon when selected.
- Button text is read by TalkBack automatically; extra labels usually unnecessary.
- Flutter map: `FilledButton`, `FilledButton.tonal`, `OutlinedButton`, `TextButton`, `ElevatedButton`; `IconButton.filled` with `isSelected` for toggles.

## Floating action button (FAB)
Source: .../components/FloatingActionButton.md, .../components/ExtendedFloatingActionButton.md
- A FAB is the primary (most common) action of a screen, in front of all content.
- Sizes: small (compact screens or visual continuity; deprecated in M3 Expressive), regular, medium ("most recommended" for compact/medium windows), large (prominent action, best for expanded windows). Choose by visual hierarchy.
- Needs a content description; extended FAB text is read automatically.
- A snackbar must not cover the FAB (the Views library shifts the FAB automatically).
- Flutter map: `FloatingActionButton` / `.small` / `.large` / `.extended`. One per screen (project judgment).

## Cards
Source: .../components/Card.md
- A card contains content and actions about a single subject. Three variants: elevated, filled, outlined.
- "Each provides the same legibility and functionality, so the type you use depends on style alone."
- Separation from background: elevated (shadow; more than filled, less than outlined), filled (subtle, least emphasis), outlined (visual boundary, greatest emphasis).
- Mobile defaults: filled/outlined elevation 0dp (dragged 8dp); elevated 1dp (dragged 2dp). Recommended card margin on mobile: 8dp.
- States: checked (check icon + foreground change), dragged. Draggable cards need an accessible alternative action.
- Card contents (images, etc.) follow their own accessibility rules.
- Flutter map: `Card.filled`, `Card.outlined`, `Card` (elevated).

## Lists
Source: .../components/List.md (Lists.md returned 404; the file is List.md)
- Continuous vertical index of text or images. Sizes: one-line, two-line, three-line (maximum lines of text).
- Do: help users find and act on an item; order logically (alphabetical, numerical); keep items short and scannable; show icons, text and actions in a consistent format.
- Purely decorative elements in an item must not take separate focus nor be announced by TalkBack (no separate click action, no extra information).
- Swipe-to-reveal (expressive): do not leave an item fully swiped; the triggered primary action must match the growing revealed element (clarity and a11y).
- Optional divider between items (see Divider).
- Flutter map: `ListTile` (1-3 lines), `ListView.builder` for long lists.

## Chips
Source: .../components/Chip.md
- Compact elements for an input, attribute, or action. Four variants: assist, filter, input, suggestion.
- Choose by purpose and author: action (assist) or filter results (filter)? Content generated by the product (suggestion) or by the user (input)?
- Filter chips: tags or descriptive words to filter content; a good alternative to toggle buttons or checkboxes; show a check when selected.
- Assist: smart/automated actions. Suggestion: dynamically generated suggestions (replies, search filters). Input: discrete user info (entity/text) turned into chips.
- Elevated style only on backgrounds needing protection (e.g. an image).
- Buttons appear consistently with calls to action; chips appear dynamically as a group of interactive elements.
- Groups: chips are most commonly used in groups; multiple rows by default or a single scrollable row; single vs multi selection; optionally require at least one selected.
- Default min height 32dp; the touch target is extended to the minimum (48dp) by default.
- Flutter map: `FilterChip`, `ChoiceChip` (single selection), `ActionChip`, `InputChip`, `Wrap` or horizontal `ListView` for groups. Keep a 48dp hit target (`materialTapTargetSize`).

## Top app bar
Source: .../components/TopAppBar.md
- Placed at the top to help navigate. Variants: search, small, medium flexible, large flexible; plus a contextual action bar for selected items (close button instead of navigation icon).
- Small: dense layouts or when the page is scrolled. Medium/large: larger headline that collapses into a small bar on scroll; large emphasizes the page headline. Non-flexible medium/large are deprecated in M3 Expressive.
- Search app bar: emphasized entry point to search, on home pages when search is key.
- Lift on scroll: same elevation as content at rest, raises when content scrolls behind it. May hide on scroll up and reappear on scroll down.
- Let the bar grow when the system font size increases (wrap content with a minimum height).
- Set content descriptions for the navigation icon, action items and overflow items.
- Edge-to-edge: bar color may flow into the status bar; add the inset to avoid overlap.
- Flutter map: `AppBar`, `SliverAppBar` (`.medium`/`.large`, `scrolledUnderElevation`), `SearchBar`; contextual mode = swapped `AppBar` (project judgment).

## Bottom sheets
Source: .../components/BottomSheet.md
- Secondary content anchored to the bottom. Modal (scrim, blocks the rest of the screen) vs standard (no scrim, co-exists with the main UI).
- Modal: presents a set of choices; an alternative to inline menus and simple dialogs on mobile, with more room for content, icons and actions. Dismissed by tapping outside or dragging down.
- Standard: keeps secondary content visible while the main region is scrolled or panned.
- The collapsed (peek) state needs enough height to hint at extra content.
- Drag handle (optional): min 48x48dp touch target; supports expand/collapse via accessibility services (TalkBack, Switch Access). Keep 48dp at the top for it.
- Supports predictive back and edge-to-edge insets.
- Flutter map: `showModalBottomSheet` (`showDragHandle: true`, `isScrollControlled`), `showBottomSheet` for standard, `DraggableScrollableSheet`.

## Dialogs
Source: .../components/Dialog.md
- Inform about a task; may hold critical information, require decisions, or involve multiple tasks. Two variants: basic and full-screen.
- Basic: interrupts with urgent information, details or actions: alerts, quick selection, confirmation. Anatomy: container, optional icon, title, supporting text, optional divider, buttons.
- Full-screen: a series of tasks to complete; the only dialog over which other dialogs may appear.
- A dialog is modal: it disables app functionality until confirmed, dismissed or the required action is done. "Dialogs are purposefully interruptive, so they should be used sparingly."
- A title icon needs a content description.
- Flutter map: `AlertDialog` via `showDialog`, `Dialog.fullscreen`. Destructive confirmation as `FilledButton` + `TextButton` is project judgment (the system delete confirmation is separate, see AGENTS.md).

## Snackbar
Source: .../components/Snackbar.md
- Brief messages about app processes at the bottom of the screen; inform of a process performed or about to be performed.
- Temporary; must not interrupt the experience; no input required to disappear (timeout, interaction elsewhere, or swipe away).
- May offer one action (undo an action just taken, retry a failed one) and an optional close button. The action dismisses the snackbar.
- Only one snackbar at a time; showing a new one dismisses the previous.
- Preferred over toasts because it appears in the context of the UI; anchor above a FAB or bottom navigation so it does not cover it.
- Default container elevation 6dp, margin 8dp. Text is read by TalkBack automatically.
- Durations: short, long, indefinite (indefinite only when an action must remain available: project judgment).
- Flutter map: `ScaffoldMessenger.of(context).showSnackBar(SnackBar(action: SnackBarAction(...), showCloseIcon: ...))`; `behavior: SnackBarBehavior.floating` to sit above a FAB.

## Progress indicators
Source: .../components/ProgressIndicator.md
- Express an unspecified wait time or the length of a process. Two types: linear, circular.
- Determinate: fills 0-100%, when progress and wait time are known. Indeterminate: when unknown.
- "Only one type should represent each kind of activity in an app" (a refresh shown circular must not be linear elsewhere).
- Circular can be applied directly to a surface such as a button or card.
- Default track thickness 4dp (primary indicator on primaryContainer track); thick variant 8dp with 4dp corner radius for a fully rounded track (expressive). A non-text contrast update keeps the indicator distinguishable from its track.
- Wavy shape (expressive) suits longer processes and increases component height.
- Flutter map: `LinearProgressIndicator(value: null | x)`, `CircularProgressIndicator`; add `semanticsLabel`/`semanticsValue` (project judgment).

## Tabs
Source: .../components/Tabs.md
- Organize content across screens, data sets and interactions. Primary tabs: top of the content pane under an app bar, main destinations. Secondary tabs: within a content area to separate related content.
- Fixed tabs: all visible with equal width. Scrollable tabs: some stay off-screen.
- Optional icon and badge; add content descriptions to tabs and badges.
- Flutter map: `TabBar` (`isScrollable`) + `TabBarView`, `Badge`.

## Navigation drawer
Source: .../components/NavigationDrawer.md
- Access to destinations. Standard (tablet/desktop, beside content; not suitable for mobile) vs modal (scrim; mobile).
- Deprecated in M3 Expressive; use an expanded navigation rail instead.
- The opening control must be a labeled 48dp target; items and scrim close it; support closing via keyboard (esc).
- Dividers appear between groups; sub-menus act as subtitles.
- Si {{NOMBRE_PROYECTO}} utiliza un navigation drawer o menú lateral, sigue esta guía al implementarlo o modificarlo, y no agregues destinos nuevos salvo que `docs/` lo requiera.
- Flutter map: `NavigationDrawer`; `NavigationRail` (extended) for large windows.

## Divider
Source: .../components/Divider.md
- Thin lines that group content in lists or containers; one way to group and create hierarchy; can imply nested parent/child relationships.
- Full width: separates larger sections of unrelated content and interactive from non-interactive areas. Inset: separates related content within a section.
- Thickness 1dp (regular), 8dp (heavy: a visual break between major sections of a long page).
- Decorative: no special accessibility precautions.
- Flutter map: `Divider`/`VerticalDivider` (`indent`, `endIndent`, `thickness`), color `outlineVariant`. Spacing first, divider second (project judgment, already in SKILL.md).
